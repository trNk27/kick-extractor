KickExtractor

Drop a hardstyle track. Every kick gets lined up and averaged until only the kick is left. Runs entirely in your browser; the file never leaves your device.

Working…

These groups' hits don't agree well with each other (e.g. kicks buried under vocals or screeches), so their averages are mushy.

How it works
  1. Find the kicks. Look for sudden jumps in the spectrum, estimate the tempo, and keep only hits that sit on the beat grid. That skips off-beat sounds. Hits with hardly any bass (chord changes in the breakdown) are dropped.
  2. Line them up. Every hit is shifted by a few samples so it matches the median hit exactly (cross-correlation).
  3. Group them. Hardstyle kicks are often re-pitched to follow the chords, kicks on 2 & 4 may have a clap on top, and melodic sections have a softer kick under a sub bass. Hits are clustered by their spectrum, groups that end up sounding the same are merged, and the loudest consistent group (usually the drop kick) comes first.
  4. Average. Taking the sample-by-sample median over all aligned hits cancels everything that isn't locked to the kick: melody, vocals, screeches.
  5. Split. In rawstyle each beat is "kick, short gap, off-beat sound (reverse bass)". If that gap is found, you get the kick and the off-beat part as separate files too.

Limits: you get the kick as it sounds in the finished master (after limiting and sidechain), not the producer's dry sample. Anything that hits exactly with the kick every time (a layered clap, a reverse bass right behind it) stays in. The "cleanest single hits" are untouched original hits, often from a kick-only intro, and sometimes sound better than the average.

Using the results: great for learning and reference. Releasing tracks with kicks ripped from someone else's song needs permission.
